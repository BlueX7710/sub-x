Sub-X privacy policy

For the Sub-X website (bluex7710.github.io/sub-x), the Sub-X Android app and the Sub-X page on claude.ai. Last updated 21 November 2026.

The short version

Sub-X has no ads and no analytics. Your times, sessions and settings are stored on your own device. They leave it only if you sign in with Google to sync them between your devices. If you subscribe to Sub-X Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play or Paddle, and RevenueCat keeps a record of your subscription.

What is stored, and where

  • Your solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Backups are files you choose to save, and they stay wherever you put them.
  • Sync (optional): if you sign in with Google, a copy of your saved data is kept in Google Firebase under your account so your devices can share it. Only you can read it. Sign out and it stops; ask us (see Contact) to delete the copy.

Sub-X Pro subscriptions

  • Sub-X Pro needs you to sign in with Google, so your subscription works on every device you use.
  • In the Android app, Google Play takes the payment. On the website, Paddle takes the payment (card, PayPal or Apple Pay) as the seller of record. Sub-X never sees or stores your card or PayPal details.
  • RevenueCat keeps the subscription record: an account id (your Google sign-in's Firebase id), which plan you have, when it started, renews or ends, and your email if you give it at checkout. Sub-X uses this only to know whether Pro is on.
  • Cancel any time in Google Play (app) or from Sub-X Pro → Manage subscription (website). See RevenueCat's, Paddle's and Google's privacy policies.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: only when you connect a Stackmat timer. Sub-X reads the timer's data signal from the microphone input to get your time. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 11 and older, location): only to find and connect a smart cube. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning on older versions; Sub-X does not use your location.
  • Files: only when you save a backup.

Things you send yourself

  • Share links contain a scramble, so whoever opens the link sees it.
  • Send to another device packs your saved data into a link or a backup file. Anyone you give that link or file to can read the times in it, so send it only to yourself or people you trust. The data sits after the "#" in the link, and browsers don't send that part to the website's server.
  • AI review (claude.ai page) sends the clip you pick to Claude through your own Claude account, under Anthropic's terms.

Other services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device downloads files from them. The Android app loads the same website. Sub-X itself doesn't collect anything beyond the sync and subscription records above.

Children

Sub-X is meant for people 13 and over. Without signing in it collects nothing from anyone. Sync and Sub-X Pro need a Google account.

Deleting your Sub-X account and data

Your Sub-X account is your Google sign-in, used for sync and Sub-X Pro. To delete it:

  1. If you have Sub-X Pro, cancel it first: in Google Play (Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions), or from Sub-X Pro → Manage subscription on the website.
  2. In Sub-X, open Account and sign out on each device.
  3. Ask for deletion by opening an issue titled "Delete my Sub-X account" at github.com/BlueX7710/sub-x/issues, or by emailing the developer address shown on Sub-X's Google Play listing. Say which Google account you used, but don't post your email address publicly.

Within 30 days we delete your synced copy in Google Firebase (solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings) and your Firebase sign-in record, and ask RevenueCat to delete your subscription record. Google Play and Paddle keep their own purchase records as tax law requires. Times saved on your devices stay there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
