Sub-X privacy policy

For the Sub-X website (bluex7710.github.io/sub-x), the Sub-X Android app and the Sub-X page on claude.ai. Last updated 31 October 2026.

The short version

Sub-X has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your times, sessions and settings are stored on your own device and are not sent to us or anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Your solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Backups are files you choose to save, and they stay wherever you put them.
  • Recordings of solves (if you turn the camera on) stay on your device unless you save or share a clip yourself.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only when you turn on solve recording. Video stays on your device.
  • Microphone: only when you connect a Stackmat timer. Sub-X reads the timer's data signal from the microphone input to get your time. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 11 and older, location): only to find and connect a smart cube. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning on older versions; Sub-X does not use your location.
  • Files: only when you save a backup or a recording.

Things you send yourself

  • Share links contain a scramble, so whoever opens the link sees it.
  • Send to another device packs your saved data into a link or a backup file. Anyone you give that link or file to can read the times in it, so send it only to yourself or people you trust. The data sits after the "#" in the link, and browsers don't send that part to the website's server.
  • AI review (claude.ai page) sends the clip you pick to Claude through your own Claude account, under Anthropic's terms.

Other services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device downloads files from them. The Android app loads the same website. Sub-X itself doesn't collect anything.

Children

Sub-X doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/BlueX7710/sub-x/issues.
