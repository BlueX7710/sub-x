Sub-X privacy policy

For the Sub-X website (bluex7710.github.io/sub-x), the Sub-X Android app and the Sub-X page on claude.ai. Last updated 22 November 2026.

The short version

Sub-X has no ads and no analytics. Your times, sessions and settings are stored on your own device. If you choose to sign in with Google to sync them between devices, a copy is kept in your own Sub-X sync storage (Google Firebase) that only your account can read. Nothing is sold or shared.

What is stored, and where

  • Your solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Backups are files you choose to save, and they stay wherever you put them.

Sign-in and sync (optional, website and Android app)

  • Sign-in uses Google's sign-in through Google Firebase Authentication. Sub-X receives your Google account's ID, name, email address and profile picture; it shows the picture on the Account button and uses the ID to find your synced data.
  • While you are signed in, everything a backup holds (solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings) is copied to Google Cloud Firestore under your account and kept in step with your other devices. Firebase's security rules let only your signed-in account read or change it.
  • Signing out stops syncing; the times on each device stay there. To have your synced copy and sign-in record deleted, open an issue at the address under Contact.
  • Google processes this data as Firebase's provider, under the Firebase privacy terms.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: only when you connect a Stackmat timer. Sub-X reads the timer's data signal from the microphone input to get your time. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 11 and older, location): only to find and connect a smart cube. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning on older versions; Sub-X does not use your location.
  • Files: only when you save a backup.

Things you send yourself

  • Share links contain a scramble, so whoever opens the link sees it.
  • Send to another device packs your saved data into a link or a backup file. Anyone you give that link or file to can read the times in it, so send it only to yourself or people you trust. The data sits after the "#" in the link, and browsers don't send that part to the website's server.
  • AI review (claude.ai page) sends the clip you pick to Claude through your own Claude account, under Anthropic's terms.

Other services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device downloads files from them. The Android app loads the same website. Sub-X itself doesn't collect anything.

Children

Sub-X doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your Sub-X account and data

Your Sub-X account is your Google sign-in, used for sync. To delete it:

  1. In Sub-X, open Account and sign out on each device.
  2. Ask for deletion by opening an issue titled "Delete my Sub-X account" at github.com/BlueX7710/sub-x/issues, or by emailing the developer address shown on Sub-X's Google Play listing. Say which Google account you used, but don't post your email address publicly.

Within 30 days we delete your synced copy in Google Firebase (solves, sessions, learned cases, trainer times, achievements and settings) and your Firebase sign-in record. Times saved on your devices stay there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/BlueX7710/sub-x/issues.
